## Recently shipped

<div className="rm-card">
  <p><strong>v3.5.4 — look up any id from what Darpan already holds, and a sync pulse on Shopify orders</strong> <span className="rm-pill">Shipped · Nov 2026</span></p>
  <p className="rm-muted">The Darpan CLI finds an order id in every system from an index of what your runs and audits already read, without calling Shopify, HotWax or NetSuite, with the order's open findings. Acknowledging an audit finding no longer needs a note. A sync pulse can watch Shopify → HotWax orders on busy days.</p>
</div>

<div className="rm-card">
  <p><strong>v3.4.16 — peak periods, a sync pulse, queries, a conclusions edit page, a CLI that does what your role allows, and audit findings that read the same way every time</strong> <span className="rm-pill">Shipped · Nov 2026</span></p>
  <p className="rm-muted">Name your busy days and Darpan moves runs out of the way, holds them out of peak hours and reads HotWax and NetSuite more lightly. A sync pulse counts the orders still waiting in the other system every few hours and posts when they pass your allowance. Tenant scripts become queries: ask a question in plain words, the agent writes it over what your audits keep and runs it, and a schedule emails the answer; queries have their own page, with your pins. Conclusions get their own edit page, and the CLI can save them and sync an automation with its run. A found HotWax or Shopify record shows its status, an order on its way to NetSuite is no longer a finding, and the welcome email carries the temporary password. Regular runs look up every record their read could not confirm, the audit page opens on the last morning that was due, and audit findings carry a priority you can work through on the audit page. Each audit statement lists only the causes it can reach, and a HotWax orders side can keep exchange orders. A run that does not run sends your chat space one plain line while Darpan works on the fix, an audit re-checks orders that changed since its last read, and a finding shows each system's payment status. A saved run you start that fails sends one plain line too, and an order finding lists its lines with each system's word for them, turned on by a read option on the run's side. An audit finding can be acknowledged with a note and a reminder, a finding sends Darpan feedback in your own words, and one icon re-checks every open finding at once. A run's read options can be switched on its page after it is made. Ships with app v4.4.12; see [Release updates](/releases/updates). Every audit finding now speaks of the systems its check compares, in the same rows, and says <strong>Missing</strong> where a system has no record.</p>
</div>
