## Recently shipped

<div className="rm-card">
  <p><strong>v3.3.0 — edit emails, settings, jobs, retention, the mascot and the agent without a release</strong> <span className="rm-pill">Shipped · Nov 2026</span></p>
  <p className="rm-muted">A super admin edits every email's words and subject with a live preview, the engine's tunables, scheduled jobs, retention periods per tenant, the mascot's words, and the agent's prompts, models and prices, in the admin app or the CLI, and a deploy keeps every edit. Sign-in asks for the username first and offers saved passkeys in its autofill; the settings pages read as rows; Notifications shows where everything posts in one grid; and the text version of every email is complete. Ships with app v4.3.0; see [Release updates](/releases/updates).</p>
</div>

<div className="rm-card">
  <p><strong>v3.2.1 — sign in with a passkey alone, two-factor for everyone, and Darpan admins in every tenant</strong> <span className="rm-pill">Shipped · Nov 2026</span></p>
  <p className="rm-muted">Sign in with Touch ID, Face ID or a security key and no password. A super admin can require two-factor for everyone from a date, with reminder emails and setup at sign-in instead of lockout. A Darpan admin works in every tenant without being added to each, and the admin CLI signs in through the browser and keeps its session. Every Darpan email wears the app's look, a run result's rule picker shows rule names, and HotWax item quantity counts exchange orders with their original order. Ships with app v4.2.0; see [Release updates](/releases/updates).</p>
</div>
