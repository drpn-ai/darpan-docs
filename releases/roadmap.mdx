## Recently shipped

<div className="rm-card">
  <p><strong>v3.0.3 — the audit agent, run updates, a CLI for your agents, and tenant scripts</strong> <span className="rm-pill">Shipped · Nov 2026</span></p>
  <p className="rm-muted">An agent on the audit page answers questions about a finding and drafts changes to the audit that a person approves, rejects or reverts. Every audit run sends a short chat update to the space its settings pick and an email with a CSV per rule, and an order counts once, under its earliest broken step; findings are open or closed, each audit runs on one schedule, a running audit shows its live reads, and one finding can be verified on demand. A tenant's own AI agents use Darpan through the darpan CLI and MCP, with access granted per user. Tenants write and schedule scripts, add REST systems as data, and keep versions of their config. NetSuite and HotWax products reconcile two-way, NetSuite fulfillments are checked in HotWax by location, and Shopify and HotWax item counts line up per order. Ships with app v4.0.2; see [Release updates](/releases/updates).</p>
</div>

<div className="rm-card">
  <p><strong>v2.1.7 — audits, password reset, faster reads and retry from the failed step</strong> <span className="rm-pill">Shipped · Oct 2026</span></p>
  <p className="rm-muted">Audits check a tenant's systems every morning against plain-English statements, keep a ledger of findings that close on their own once fixed, and explain each finding with its cause, its fix and the records a person searches by. Audits can be created, played and scheduled from the app, and report by digest and over a read-only GraphQL API. People reset their own password by an emailed code. HotWax reads are shared between checks, paced to HotWax's cost limit, and a failed run can retry from the step it stopped at, reusing the reads it already made. Shopify reads a month of orders in steady memory, and a key repeated in one system is reported as a finding instead of stopping the run. Rules check lists, such as every HotWax payment reference among the order's Shopify transactions, and HotWax reads can leave out a sales channel. Ships with app v3.1.3; see [Release updates](/releases/updates).</p>
</div>
