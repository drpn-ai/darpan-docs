## Recently shipped

<div className="rm-card">
  <p><strong>v3.1.0 — two-factor sign-in, passkeys, and tenant users who run and connect</strong> <span className="rm-pill">Shipped · Nov 2026</span></p>
  <p className="rm-muted">Sign in with a code from an authenticator app or a passkey after your password, with recovery codes as the fallback, and require two-factor for admin roles from a date. Sessions end after an idle hour and within a day, and passwords need 12 characters. Tenant users run audits, resolve findings and manage connections, and a new member gets a welcome email. Audits read a seven-day Previous Week, warn when their chat space cannot be used, link to the right tenant, and name the record in fix text; the agent drafts new statements. Darpan reads Shopify customer data at Level 1 and keeps raw extracts for 7 days. Ships with app v4.1.0; see [Release updates](/releases/updates).</p>
</div>

<div className="rm-card">
  <p><strong>v3.0.3 — the audit agent, run updates, a CLI for your agents, and tenant scripts</strong> <span className="rm-pill">Shipped · Nov 2026</span></p>
  <p className="rm-muted">An agent on the audit page answers questions about a finding and drafts changes to the audit that a person approves, rejects or reverts. Every audit run sends a short chat update to the space its settings pick and an email with a CSV per rule, and an order counts once, under its earliest broken step; findings are open or closed, each audit runs on one schedule, a running audit shows its live reads, and one finding can be verified on demand. A tenant's own AI agents use Darpan through the darpan CLI and MCP, with access granted per user. Tenants write and schedule scripts, add REST systems as data, and keep versions of their config. NetSuite and HotWax products reconcile two-way, NetSuite fulfillments are checked in HotWax by location, and Shopify and HotWax item counts line up per order. Ships with app v4.0.3; see [Release updates](/releases/updates).</p>
</div>
