## Recently shipped

<div className="rm-card">
  <p><strong>v3.2.0 — sign in with a passkey alone, two-factor for everyone, and Darpan admins in every tenant</strong> <span className="rm-pill">Shipped · Nov 2026</span></p>
  <p className="rm-muted">Sign in with Touch ID, Face ID or a security key and no password. A super admin can require two-factor for everyone from a date, with reminder emails and setup at sign-in instead of lockout. A Darpan admin works in every tenant without being added to each, and the admin CLI signs in through the browser and keeps its session. Every Darpan email wears the app's look, a run result's rule picker shows rule names, and HotWax item quantity counts exchange orders with their original order. Ships with app v4.2.0; see [Release updates](/releases/updates).</p>
</div>

<div className="rm-card">
  <p><strong>v3.1.0 — two-factor sign-in, passkeys, and tenant users who run and connect</strong> <span className="rm-pill">Shipped · Nov 2026</span></p>
  <p className="rm-muted">Sign in with a code from an authenticator app or a passkey after your password, with recovery codes as the fallback, and require two-factor for admin roles from a date. Sessions end after an idle hour and within a day, and passwords need 12 characters. Tenant users run audits, resolve findings and manage connections, and a new member gets a welcome email. Audits read a seven-day Previous Week, warn when their chat space cannot be used, link to the right tenant, and name the record in fix text; the agent drafts new statements. Darpan reads Shopify customer data at Level 1 and keeps raw extracts for 7 days. Ships with app v4.1.0; see [Release updates](/releases/updates).</p>
</div>
