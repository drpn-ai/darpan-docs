## Recently shipped

<div className="rm-card">
  <p><strong>v3.4.4 — peak periods, a sync pulse, queries, and a conclusions edit page</strong> <span className="rm-pill">Shipped · Nov 2026</span></p>
  <p className="rm-muted">Name your busy days and Darpan moves runs out of the way, holds them out of peak hours and reads HotWax and NetSuite more lightly. A sync pulse counts the orders still waiting in the other system every few hours and posts when they pass your allowance. Tenant scripts become queries: ask a question in plain words, the agent writes it over what your audits keep and runs it, and a schedule emails the answer; queries have their own page, with your pins. Conclusions get their own edit page, and the CLI can save them and sync an automation with its run. A found HotWax or Shopify record shows its status, an order on its way to NetSuite is no longer a finding, and the welcome email carries the temporary password. Regular runs look up every record their read could not confirm, the audit page opens on the last morning that was due, and audit findings carry a priority you can work through on the audit page. Ships with app v4.4.5; see [Release updates](/releases/updates).</p>
</div>

<div className="rm-card">
  <p><strong>v3.3.2 — edit emails, settings, jobs, retention, the mascot and the agent without a release</strong> <span className="rm-pill">Shipped · Nov 2026</span></p>
  <p className="rm-muted">A super admin edits every email's words and subject with a live preview, the engine's tunables, scheduled jobs, retention periods per tenant, the mascot's words, and the agent's prompts, models and prices, in the admin app or the CLI, and a deploy keeps every edit. Sign-in asks for the username first, offers saved passkeys in its autofill, and opens on Welcome back with one passkey prompt on a browser your passkey has confirmed; the settings pages read as rows; Notifications shows where everything posts in one grid; and the text version of every email is complete. Ships with app v4.3.2; see [Release updates](/releases/updates).</p>
</div>
